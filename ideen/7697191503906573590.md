## Beschreibung

How to Automate YouTube Content With Claude AI + YouTube

Was wenn du könntest, deinen gesamten YouTube-Content-Workflow in einem Claude-Projekt zu organisieren? Statt bei jeder Videoerstellung bei null anzufangen, kannst du ein wiederholbares System für Forschung, Skripte, Shorts und Upload-Planung aufbauen.

1. TOPIC RESEARCH: Find video ideas that match your niche and audience. Use research to identify relevant questions, trends, and topics worth exploring.
2. RESEARCH & OUTLINE: Gather reliable sources, verify important facts, and organize your ideas into a clear video structure.
3. SCRIPT WRITING: Use Claude to draft engaging hooks, structured scripts, and natural transitions. Review and personalize every script before publishing.
4. SHORTS REPURPOSING: Turn suitable long-form content into Shorts scripts with strong openings and one clear takeaway per video.
5. CONTENT PLANNING: Create a realistic publishing calendar with video titles, formats, deadlines, and content ideas.
6. PUBLISHING CHECKLIST: Prepare titles, descriptions, thumbnail concepts, and upload checklists. Review everything in YouTube Studio before publishing.
7. IMPROVE THE WORKFLOW: Review your analytics, identify what viewers respond to, and use those insights to improve future videos.

How Claude Projects helfen: You can create a dedicated Claude Project with your niche, audience, preferred script format, tone, and content guidelines. Reuse those instructions for each new video instead of explaining everything again.

Important: A Claude Project does not automatically publish videos or perform every step by itself. Some tasks still require your input, other tools, or integrations. Always fact-check AI-generated content and add your own creative judgment.

## YouTube-Vergleich

- **Format-Transferabilität:** Photo-Content weniger direkt auf YouTube Shorts übertragbar; stattdessen YouTube Community-Post oder Carousel-Style.
- **SEO-Potenzial:** TikTok-Hashtags sind flüchtiger; YouTube profitiert von suchbasiertem Traffic mit längerer Halbwertszeit.
- **Monetarisierung:** YouTube-Partnerprogramm ab 1.000 Abonnenten + 10M Short-Views; TikTok-Creator-Fonds ab 10K Follower + 10K Views/30T.
- **Empfehlung:** Photo auf TikTok lassen, ggf. als Pinterest-Pin repurposen.
